Facing: 0° is north. FOV: 5°–180°, default 120°. Wheel / pinch still zooms.

Local aircraft radar

Optional view-up ATC-style scope. Choose miles or kilometres, then select a 1, 2, 5, 10 or 25 unit radius. The centre marker is your saved observer location and the radar rotates like a compass as you pan or follow objects in Live Sky. The radar can show nearby tracked aircraft even when they are below your visual horizon; Live Sky and Live Contacts remain above-horizon only. Click an aircraft blip to select it in Live Sky.

Live aircraft display

Perspective aircraft use reported heading, roll and turn rate, plus real sky motion and 0–50 mile depth scaling. Switch to dots for the lightweight simple view.
